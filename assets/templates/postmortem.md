# Postmortem: <incident>

Status: <investigating | resolved>

## Impact

Who and what was affected, for how long, and what was lost or delayed, with numbers and their sources.

## Timeline

Times, events and the evidence for each: log lines, exit-status files, ledger entries.

## Cause

The established cause with its evidence. A cause that is not established is written as "cause not established", with what was ruled out.

## Recovery

What was done to restore service or data, and the check that showed it worked.

## Prevention

Changes made so that the incident cannot recur or is detected earlier, each with its check or note.
